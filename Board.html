Executive Directives & Governance Mandate

I. The newly appointed Core Executive Board is entrusted with the full administration of BioGenius activities, events, outreach, and financial management for the 2026–2027 academic session. All office bearers are expected to uphold the highest standards of professionalism, integrity, and discipline.

II. All appointed Executive Board members are required to attend the mandatory Inaugural Strategy Meeting. Detailed schedule, venue, and agenda items will be communicated via official channels shortly. Attendance is strictly compulsory.

The Executive Board extends its gratitude to all candidates who participated in the recruitment process. With this powerful leadership Board in place, BioGenius embarks on a vision of academic excellence, innovation, and impactful student initiatives.

BY ORDER OF THE EXECUTIVE COUNCIL
